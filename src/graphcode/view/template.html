Click a node to see its code and neighbours. Double-click a box to expand or collapse it.

Legend

    • Colour: top-level module or subpackage.
    • Size: number of connections (calls, imports, inherits, containment).
    • Faint lines: a module or class holding its members.
    • Dashed ring: a folded class; double-click to show or hide its methods.
    • Hover or click a ball to light up its neighbours.
